# UTP 'TH' message type reports prior day as-of trades

The **'TH' message type** in the UTP (Unlisted Trading Privileges) Data Feed is the **Prior Day As-Of Trade Message**. This message category 'T' (Trade) with type 'H' reports trade transactions that occurred on a previous trading day but were not reported at that time, or cancels previously reported as-of trades. It appears exclusively on the UTDF (UTP Trade Data Feed), not on the quotation feed, and can be generated by all UTP participants.

## Purpose and operational context

The Prior Day As-Of Trade Message addresses a fundamental need in trade reporting: handling late trade reports and corrections for prior business days. When a UTP participant needs to add a trade that should have been reported on an earlier date, or cancel an as-of trade that was previously submitted, the TH message provides the mechanism. The critical **asOfAction** field distinguishes between these two operations—**'A' for additions** and **'C' for cancellations**.

Unlike regular trade reports that update consolidated high/low/last statistics, TH messages are informational records about prior day activity. They do not affect current-day price statistics for the consolidated tape, making them distinct from same-day trade corrections (TY messages) or cancellations (TZ messages).

## Binary message format specification

The binary format totals **84 bytes** and follows the standard UTP message structure with a 29-byte header plus message-specific fields:

| Field | Offset | Length | Type | Description |
|-------|--------|--------|------|-------------|
| version | 0 | 1 | byte | Protocol Version (currently 1) |
| msgCategory | 1 | 1 | byte | **T** – Trade Messages |
| msgType | 2 | 1 | byte | **H** – As-Of Trade Message |
| orig | 3 | 1 | byte | Market Center Originator ID |
| subMarketId | 4 | 1 | byte | Sub Market Center ID |
| sipTime | 5 | 8 | long | SIP timestamp (nanoseconds since Epoch) |
| timestamp1 | 13 | 8 | long | Participant timestamp |
| partToken | 21 | 8 | long | Participant token (internal SIP use) |
| timestamp2 | 29 | 8 | long | FINRA timestamp (0 for exchanges) |
| symbol | 37 | 11 | byte[] | Security identifier |
| tradeId | 48 | 8 | long | Trade identifier |
| price | 56 | 8 | long | Trade price (6 implied decimals) |
| volume | 64 | 4 | int | Trade volume in shares |
| cond | 68 | 4 | byte[] | Sale condition modifiers |
| tradeThrExempt | 72 | 1 | byte | Trade Through Exempt flag |
| saleDays | 73 | 2 | short | Seller's sale days (for "R" condition) |
| asOfAction | 75 | 1 | byte | **A** = Addition, **C** = Cancel |
| priorTime | 76 | 8 | long | Original trade timestamp |

## Key fields with unique behaviors

Several fields in the TH message have special characteristics worth noting for implementers:

The **tradeId** field behaves differently than in regular trade reports. Per the specification, "the tradeId for as-of trades is a pass-through value sent by the Participant. There is no guarantee of uniqueness, and may be 0 if the Participant chooses not to include a tradeId." This contrasts with same-day trades where the combination of originator, sub-market center, symbol, and tradeId is guaranteed unique within a session.

The **timestamp2** field carries FINRA timing data when trades originate from FINRA ADF or a FINRA Trade Reporting Facility. For exchange-originated trades, this field is set to **0**. When populated, it represents nanoseconds since Epoch and indicates the time of execution as reported to FINRA.

The **priorTime** field captures when the original trade actually occurred or should have been reported, providing the historical timestamp separate from the current reporting timestamp in **sipTime**.

## Code definitions for asOfAction

| Code | Value |
|------|-------|
| A | As-Of Trade Addition |
| C | As-Of Trade Cancel |

## Integration with UTP feed architecture

The TH message fits within the broader UTP Trade Data Feed ecosystem managed by the SIP (Securities Information Processor). According to the official message table, it shares category 'T' with four other trade message types: **TA/TW** (short/long trade reports), **TZ** (cancel/error), and **TY** (correction). The 'H' designator uniquely identifies prior-day operations.

All **23 UTP participants**—including Nasdaq exchanges, NYSE Group, Cboe exchanges, IEX, MEMX, MIAX Pearl, and FINRA—can generate TH messages. The Market Center Originator ID in byte 3 identifies the source, while the Sub-Market Center ID in byte 4 distinguishes FINRA Trade Reporting Facilities (NYSE TRF as 'N', Nasdaq TRF Carteret as 'Q', Nasdaq TRF Chicago as 'B').

## Conclusion

The TH message type serves the essential compliance function of maintaining accurate historical trade records when same-day reporting requirements cannot be met. Its distinct **asOfAction** field, non-guaranteed **tradeId** uniqueness, and **priorTime** timestamp differentiate it from regular trade reporting mechanisms. Implementers processing UTDF feeds should handle TH messages separately from current-day statistics while maintaining them for regulatory and audit purposes. The official specification is maintained by Nasdaq Market Technology in the UTP Data Feed Services Specification Binary document, currently at version 1.9a (revised June 2025).
